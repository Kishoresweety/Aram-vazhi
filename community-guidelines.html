Community Guidelines

These guidelines help us maintain a respectful, inclusive, and productive community for everyone.

Be Respectful

Treat every community member with dignity and respect. Disagreements are natural but should be handled constructively and with empathy. Personal attacks, insults, and disrespectful language have no place in our community.

Stay On Topic

Keep discussions relevant to digital welfare, technology ethics, cybersecurity, media literacy, and related areas. Off-topic content, spam, or unsolicited promotional messages disrupt the quality of our community conversations.

Share Knowledge Freely

Aram Vazhi thrives on the open exchange of ideas and knowledge. Share resources, research, and insights generously. Credit original authors and sources appropriately to maintain trust and intellectual integrity within our community.

Protect Privacy

Never share personal information about yourself or others without explicit consent. Be mindful of what you post — once shared, information can be difficult to fully control. Respect the digital boundaries of all community members at all times.

Report Issues

If you witness harassment, misinformation, or unethical behavior within our community spaces, please report it to the team promptly. We rely on community members to help maintain the integrity and safety of our shared platforms.

Support New Members

Everyone starts somewhere. Experienced members are encouraged to welcome newcomers, answer questions patiently, and foster a culture of learning, inclusion, and mutual growth across all experience levels.

No Spam or Self-Promotion

Unsolicited promotional content, repeated messages, or irrelevant links are not permitted in our community spaces. All contributions should add genuine value, insight, or learning opportunities to fellow members.
